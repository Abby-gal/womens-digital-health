Need Help?

Understanding changes in your health can sometimes feel overwhelming. Digital health tools can help you organise information and prepare forhealthcare appointments, but they cannot replace professional medical care.

If you are experiencing new, persistent or worsening symptoms, consider speaking with a qualified healthcare professional who can assess your individual situation.

Prepare for Your Healthcare Appointment

Preparing information before an appointment can make it easier to explain what you have been experiencing. A notes app, symptom tracker or digital health diary can help you organise important information over time.

Track Your Symptoms

Record when symptoms occur, how long they last and whether they are changing over time. For menstrual symptoms, this could include bleeding, pain, fatigue and changes in your cycle.

Keep Your Health Information Together

Keep an up-to-date record of medications, allergies, previous diagnoses and relevant treatments. Having this information available digitally can make it easier to provide accurate details during a healthcare appointment.

Prepare Questions in Advance

Write down questions you would like to discuss before your appointment. This could include questions about symptoms, possible investigations, treatment options or changes you have noticed in your health.

Bring Relevant Health Records

If available, bring relevant test results, imaging reports or previous medical documents to your appointment. Patient portals and digital health records can make it easier to access and organise this information.

When to Seek Medical Help

Some symptoms require urgent medical attention. Seek prompt medical care if you experience symptoms such as very heavy bleeding, severe or sudden pelvic pain, fainting, severe dizziness or difficulty breathing.

If you believe you are experiencing a medical emergency, contact your local emergency medical service immediately.
