Women's health Conditions

Women's health includes conditions that can affect quality of life, fertility and overall well-being. Digital health technologies can help women access information , track symptoms and communicate important health information with healthcare professionals.

Uterine Fibroids

Uterine Fibroids are non-cancerous growths that develop in or around the uterus. They can cause symptoms such as heavy menstrual bleeding, pelvic pain and pressure.

How Digital Health Can Help

Digital health tools can help women track heavy menstrual bleeding, pelvic pain, fatigue and other symptoms over time. Digital symptom diaries can make it easier to identify patterns and provide healthcare professionals with more structured information during consultations.

Polyendocrine Metabolic Ovarian Syndrome (PMOS)

Polyendocrine Metabolic Ovarian Syndrome (PMOS), formerly known as Polycystic Ovary Syndrome (PCOS), was renamed in 2026 to better reflect the broader hormonal and metabolic nature of the condition. PMOS can affect the menstrual cycle, fertility and overall health. Symptoms can include irregular periods, acne, excess hair growth and metabolic changes.

How Digital Health Can Help

Digital health tools can help women with PMOS monitor menstrual cycles, symptoms and changes in their health over time. Tracking tools can also support the monitoring of lifestyle factors such as physical activity, nutrition and sleep.

By bringing this information together, digital platforms can help women recognise patterns and prepare more structured information for healthcare appointments. They may also support medication reminders, treatment monitoring and access to reliable information about the condition.

Endometriosis

Endometriosis is a chronic condition in which tissue similar to the lining of the uterus grows outside the uterus. It can cause symptoms such as severe pelvic pain, painful periods and fertility problems. Digital health tools may support symptom tracking and help patients document changes in their health over time.

How Digital Health Can Help

Digital health tools can help women with endometriosis record pain, menstrual symptoms and their impact on everyday life. Tracking symptoms over time can help identify recurring patterns and provide more detailed information for healthcare consultations.

Digital platforms can also help patients document medication use and treatment response, prepare for appointments and access reliable health information. Remote support and digital communication may also help patients stay connected with healthcare professionals between appointments.
