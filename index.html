Women's Digital Health

Understanding women's health through information, technology and digital healthcare.

Digital health has the potential to improve how women understand and manage their health. Technologies such as mobile health applications, symptom tracking tools and digital communication platforms can help women document their health experiences, access reliable information and communicate more effectively with healthcare professionals.

How Digital Health Can Support Women's Health

Digital health can support women at different stages of their healthcare journey. It can help with earlier recognition of symptoms, monitoring changes over time and preparing useful information for healthcare appointments.

Digital tools may also support treatment monitoring, patient education and communication with healthcare professionals. By giving women better access to their own health information, digital healthcare can support more informed and active participation in their care.
